CLEAR

Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026

The short version

CLEAR is a budgeting and debt-payoff tool for one or two people. It has no accounts, no logins, and no advertising. We do not sell, rent, or share your information, and there are no analytics or tracking services in the app.

The only information CLEAR holds is the budget information you type in yourself, stored so that it syncs between your own devices.

Who is responsible

CLEAR is operated by the individual publisher of the app ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, contact trey@pgrooves.com.

What CLEAR stores

Everything below is information you enter or import yourself:

  • Household setup — whether you use the app alone or with a partner, the first names or labels you choose, and your tithe percentage.
  • Budget setup — income sources, bills and fixed expenses, savings categories, spending categories, and the amounts and due dates you assign them.
  • Transactions — date, amount, category, optional note, whether it was cash or credit, which credit card label you selected, which person it was attributed to, and the month it belongs to.
  • Debts — the label, balance, original balance, minimum payment, and interest rate you enter, plus your chosen payoff strategy.
  • Credit card labels — the names you give your cards. CLEAR never asks for and never stores card numbers, expiry dates, or security codes.
  • Pinned balances — an account balance figure you type in manually, with the date you entered it. This is a snapshot you maintain by hand; it is not fetched from any bank.
  • Import settings — the layout of statements you have imported before, and a private list matching merchant names to your categories, so future imports can suggest categories.
  • Your household code — stored locally on your device so you do not have to re-enter it.

What CLEAR does not collect

  • No email address, password, or user account.
  • No bank logins, bank connections, or card numbers. CLEAR never connects to a financial institution.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No analytics, telemetry, usage profiling, or crash-reporting services.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.
  • No purchase or payment processing of any kind.

How your information is used

Your information is used for one purpose only: to operate the app for you — to calculate what you have left to spend, track your categories and debts, and keep the same information in step across the devices in your household. It is not used for advertising, profiling, resale, or training any model.

Importing bank statements

CLEAR can read a CSV file you choose, or text you paste from your bank's website or a statement. That file or text is parsed entirely inside your own browser. The file itself is never uploaded anywhere. Only the resulting transaction rows — the ones you review and choose to import — are saved to your household, exactly as if you had typed them in by hand.

Where your information is stored

Your household information is stored using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, a hosting service provided by Google. Google processes and stores this data on our behalf as a service provider, under Google's own security and privacy commitments. Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy documentation.

To protect against automated abuse, the app may also use Google reCAPTCHA and Firebase App Check to verify that requests come from the genuine app. Where enabled, this involves Google receiving technical device and request information for that security check.

Google Firebase is the only third party that receives your information. We do not share it with anyone else.

How household codes work — please read

CLEAR deliberately has no logins, so that logging a purchase takes seconds. Access to a household is controlled by its household code instead.

Anyone who has your household code can view and change that household's budget information. Treat the code like a key. Share it only with the person you share the household with.

Codes are generated using your device's cryptographic random number generator and are ten characters long, drawn from a 32-character alphabet that omits easily confused characters. That is roughly one quadrillion possible codes, so a code cannot realistically be guessed. Our database rules also prevent anyone from listing or enumerating households; a household can only be opened by someone who already knows its exact code.

Because there are no accounts, there is also no password reset and no way for us to restore access. If you lose your household code, the information in that household cannot be recovered. Keep a copy somewhere safe.

How long information is kept

Your household information is kept until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry. Because there are no accounts, we have no way to identify who a given household belongs to, and we do not monitor or review household contents.

Deleting your information

You are in control of deletion, and you can do all of it from inside the app:

  • Delete selected transactions — check them in the transaction list and use Delete Logs.
  • Delete all transactions — use Clear Log at the bottom of the transaction list.
  • Erase the whole household back to zero — use Start From Scratch in Settings. This wipes transactions, debts, cards, and amounts.
  • Remove the code from a device — use Sign Out in Settings. This clears the code from that device only and leaves the household intact for your partner.

If you would like the underlying household record deleted entirely from storage, email trey@pgrooves.com with your household code and we will delete that record. Please note that sending us the code lets us open the household in order to delete it, and that deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising them. Because CLEAR stores no identifiers, you already have direct access to everything held about you: it is all visible and editable inside the app, and you can delete it yourself at any time using the steps above. If you need help with any request, contact us at the address above.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Children

CLEAR is intended for adults managing a household budget. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has entered personal information into a household, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored by Google Firebase with encryption at rest. Access is restricted by database rules that block enumeration and deletion by clients. No system is perfectly secure, and because access depends on your household code, keeping that code private is an essential part of protecting your information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect. Continuing to use CLEAR after an update means you accept the revised policy.
